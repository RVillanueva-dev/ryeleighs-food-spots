About Me

Ryeleigh Villanueva

Hi! I'm Ryeleigh, a student who enjoys discovering and trying different food spots. I created this guide to share Filipino food places that I enjoy and recommend to others.

I chose this topic because Filipino food is a big part of our culture and everyday life. I want to share food spots that are affordable, satisfying, and worth trying, especially for students and other food lovers.

My recommendations are based on my personal experience and honest opinions about the food. I focus on places that I believe offer good food and a satisfying experience.
